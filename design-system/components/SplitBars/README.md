How a crowd split across a few choices: one row per choice with a bar and its share, the player's pick marked "Your pick". Bars grow in on mount.

**Consumer provides** `items` (`{ label, count, mine? }`) and a game `color`. Shares are worked out from the counts.

- Only real crowd data, and only once enough people have answered (20 or more); otherwise say how many answers there are so far.
- Up to six rows; keep labels short.
