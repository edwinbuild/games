A player's icon on a disc of their colour, used in the top bar, on boards, in friends lists and in online rooms. Drawn in the house style: ink outlines, white fills.

**Consumer provides** an `icon` id and a `colour` (a game colour token), a `size` in px, and a `label` when the icon is the only thing naming the player.

- 25 icons are open to every signed-in player (the ticket is the default); 6 are earned: flame (7-day streak), crown (30 days), trophy (100 games), comet (20 different games), balloons (3 friends), medal (first on a daily board).
- Unknown icons fall back to the ticket. Guests have no icon.
